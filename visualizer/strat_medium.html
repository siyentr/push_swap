Medium strategy, op by op

A replay of sort_medium: a sliding index window of width n / ⌊√n⌋ sends A into B as a rough “butterfly”, then the largest value in B is rotated to the top and pushed back until A is sorted. Each bar's length is its rank.

Reading it

Window [low, high] drawn on A's length axis: a top bar ending inside it gets pushed.
Outlined bar: top of A while pushing, max of B while pulling.
Small ranks (≤ low) go to the bottom of B with rb, so B ends up roughly largest-in-the-middle and each max is close to the top.
